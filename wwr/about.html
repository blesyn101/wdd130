Whitewater Rafting

At Whitewater Rafting Company, we connect adventure seekers with the raw power and beauty of the river. Our purpose is to provide safe, exhilarating, and unforgettable white water experiences that challenge your limits, build lasting bonds, and foster a deep respect for our natural waterways. Whether you are navigating your first class II rapid or tackling a roaring class V drop, we guide you every splash of the way.

History

Founded in 2010 by a small group of passionate river guides, Whitewater Rafting Company started with just two secondhand rafts and a shared dream of exploring the backcountry. What began as a weekend passion project quickly evolved into a premier outdoor adventure outfit. Over the past nearly two decades, we have expanded our fleet, certified hundreds of expert guides, and safely navigated thousands of miles of roaring rapids. Despite our growth, our core philosophy remains unchanged: to deliver the ultimate river rush while preserving the wild ecosystems we call home
